## Overview

I'm a multi-time USA junior (15-18) national champion and [national record holder](https://usacycling.org/resources/national-champions-records/national-records).

I raced internationally in the Netherlands and Canada as a sprinter on the USA Cycling Olympic Development Program (ODP). I had just qualified for the 2020 Junior World Championships in Cairo, Egypt when the pandemic hit the US. I left cycling shortly after to pursue my passion for software development.
